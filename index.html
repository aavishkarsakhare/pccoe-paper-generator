📘 About PCCOE Exam Generator

Pimpri Chinchwad College Of Engineering, Pune presents a smart examination platform for faculty and students.

✨ Features:

  • 📂 Excel import/export with full LaTeX math support (matrices, integrals, derivatives)
  • 📊 Question buckets with Easy/Medium/Hard difficulty
  • 🎯 Blueprint-based random paper generation
  • 🔁 Real-time session publishing (Active/Expired)
  • 📑 Beautiful PDF generation with student details and rendered equations
  • 📜 Template saving & session history
